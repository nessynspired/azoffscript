Fix video cropping on This Week tab assignment cards

The "Your Part" cards on the This Week tab were cropping videos:

1. Uploaded videos were wrapped in max-h-[130px] overflow-hidden
   with aspect-video (16:9), which badly cropped vertical (9:16)
   videos. Replaced with max-h-[200px] and object-contain so the
   full video shows without being cut off.

2. Added object-contain to VideoPlayer's default classes so
   vertical videos are always letterboxed (full video visible)
   rather than cropped to fill a container.

3. ClipDetailModal — removed aspect-video constraint on uploaded
   videos so vertical videos display properly in the modal too.

The compact TikTok embed fix from the previous commit (360px
wrapper height) also applies to these cards.
