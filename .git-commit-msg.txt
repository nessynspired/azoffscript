Add Recipe Builder — planner builds production packs without seeing IP

The planner can now build a full production pack (recipe) for each
clip — goal, creator task, prompt, video parts, recording
instructions, admin editing recipe — by picking from a blind bank
of Shot Recipes, Recording Styles, and Transitions. She never sees
the actual library pages or IP content.
