Fix TikTok embed sizing — constrain container to video width

ROOT CAUSE:
  The TikTok embed iframe was hardcoded to maxWidth: 380px (the
  TikTok player's natural width) with margin: 0 auto, but the
  outer container expanded to full width. This created a large
  dark blue box with a small 380px video centered inside it —
  the "video in a small corner of the blue container" issue.

  On the Watch tab (cards ~400px wide) this meant dark padding
  on both sides of the video. In the ClipDetailModal (672px
  wide) the video looked tiny in the middle of a huge dark box.

FIX:
  Constrain the SocialEmbed outer container to match the video
  width (380px full, 340px compact) with mx-auto centering.
  The dark background now only covers the video area, not the
  full card width. The video fills its container properly.

  Also removed the redundant bg-desert-night/10 wrapper on the
  Watch tab that was adding a second layer of blue around the
  already-sized embed.
