Add Calendar Builder view to Ready Bank — 3-panel planning workspace

The Ready Bank now has a toggle between List View (the existing
template browser) and Calendar Builder View (a new 3-panel workspace).

Calendar Builder layout:
  LEFT: Ready Bank templates (compact, searchable, click to select)
  CENTER: Big calendar (week/month toggle) showing scheduled clips
  RIGHT: Libraries panel with 6 tabs:
    - Hooks (from growth hook library)
    - Prompts (from growth prompt intelligence)
    - Captions (from growth caption frameworks)
    - Transitions (from transition library)
    - Recording Styles (from recording style library)
    - Shot Recipes (from shot recipe library)

When you select a Ready Bank template on the left, the right panel
automatically shows matching library items — hooks that work with
that format, prompts for that category, caption frameworks that fit,
etc. This is the "put together from libraries" part.

Click "Schedule" on the selected template to open the date picker
modal with crew assignment, deadlines, and goes-live date — same
flow as the existing Ready Bank scheduling, but integrated into
the calendar workspace.
