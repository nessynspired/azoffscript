Add AZ Off Script Growth Intelligence System (9 master documents)

The previous Discovery Library was a single page with captions and
hashtags. The user requested a complete restructuring into 9 separate
master documents — a true Growth Intelligence System that protects the
brand identity while driving discovery.

The question is not 'What is trending?'
The question is 'What is trending that we can make uniquely AZ Off Script?'

New /portal/growth hub page links all 9 documents:

DOCUMENT 1: Market Gap Intelligence (src/lib/growth/market-gap-intelligence.ts)
  3 parts: Saturated Markets (16), White Space Opportunities (8),
  Anti-Brand Rules (10). For each saturated market: what everyone does,
  why it's a problem, what AZ Off Script does instead, best formats,
  and example prompts. White space has moats and success metrics.

DOCUMENT 2: Hook Library (src/lib/growth/hook-library.ts)
  88 hooks across 8 types: Curiosity, Debate, Identity, Emotional,
  Pattern Interrupt, Local, Story, Challenge. Each hook has an effect
  description, delivery method (on-screen/spoken/either), and best
  formats. Not trends — hooks that work repeatedly.

DOCUMENT 3: Caption Frameworks (src/lib/growth/caption-framework-library.ts)
  10 frameworks, not individual captions. Each has a formula, purpose,
  why it works, examples, best formats, and what to avoid. The formulas
  stay permanent; the examples rotate.

DOCUMENT 4: Prompt Intelligence (src/lib/growth/prompt-intelligence.ts)
  60+ prompts across 14 categories: Women, Men, Dating, Friendship,
  Arizona, Parenting, Adulting, Deep Conversations, Texting, Family,
  Beauty/Style, Public Behavior, Cultural, Work/Money. Each has a
  sub-topic and best formats. The massive idea engine.

DOCUMENT 5: Search Keyword Intelligence (src/lib/growth/search-keyword-intelligence.ts)
  50+ keywords across 8 categories with performance tracking
  (High/Medium/Low/New/Fading) and related keywords.

DOCUMENT 6: Trend Capture System (src/lib/growth/trend-capture-system.ts)
  13 trends with full evaluation: why it works, why people watch,
  AZ Off Script adaptation, best formats, and a Keep/Modify/Ignore
  decision. Stage tracking: Rising/Peaking/Fading/Watch.

DOCUMENT 7: Seasonal Opportunities (src/lib/growth/seasonal-opportunity-library.ts)
  16 opportunities across 7 seasons with prompt ideas, best formats,
  and timing notes.

DOCUMENT 8: Do Not Chase Rules (src/lib/growth/do-not-chase-rules.ts)
  12 rules with severity (Brand Killer/Diluter/Distractor). Each has
  what to avoid, why, what to do instead, and specific behaviors.

DOCUMENT 9: Brand Moat Library (src/lib/growth/brand-moat-library.ts)
  10 moats across 8 types. Each moat has: what it is, why no one can
  copy it, how we protect it, and examples. This answers the biggest
  question: 'Why should someone choose AZ Off Script instead of the
  thousands of other pages?'
