Admins bypass all term locks — no gate, no banner, no drop lock

Admins were being blocked by the same terms gates as regular crew
members. After bumping Quick Terms to v2, the admin was locked out
of the Drop page and saw the "Agree to the room rules first" lock
screen — even though they're the brand owner and shouldn't be bound
by their own terms.

Three places now exempt admins:

1. TermsGate (first-login welcome + quick terms modal) — admins skip
   the entire flow. No welcome popup, no terms checkbox modal. They
   go straight into the portal.

2. QuickTermsBanner (sticky "you haven't agreed" reminder) — admins
   never see the banner.

3. Drop page (terms lock + clip lock) — admins can drop links, clips,
   and ideas without accepting quick terms or signing the Creator
   Release. Both `termsLocked` and `clipLocked` are false for admins.

The quick-terms page itself is still accessible to admins if they
want to review the rules — they just aren't forced to accept.
